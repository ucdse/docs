# Competitive Analysis Matrix

This document provides a comparative analysis of our Dublin ML App against several prominent bike-sharing systems across Europe. This research was conducted during Sprint 1 to identify market gaps and define our unique value propositions.

| Feature | Your Project (Dublin ML App) | Dublin Bikes (Official JCDecaux App) | Villo! (Brussels) | Vélib' Métropole (Paris) | Bicing / Smou (Barcelona) | Santander Cycles (London) |

No future prediction available. | **Real-Time + Heatmap:** Shows "High/Low" traffic zones to guide users to balanced areas. | **Predictive Probability:** Shows "High/Med/Low" probability of finding a bike in 15-30 mins. | **Traffic Light System:** Uses Green/Amber/Red icons to indicate simple availability levels. |
